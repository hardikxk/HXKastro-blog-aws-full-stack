> “A log line is a message to someone who has arrived late.”

The best diagnostic output is an argument for what happened. It carries enough context to let a future reader rebuild the situation, without making them excavate a mountain of unrelated facts.

## Make the event recognizable

Start with the operation, then the subject, then the outcome. Identifiers are useful only after the reader knows what they identify.

```txt
cache.refresh  user=42  outcome=miss  source=profile-api  elapsed=126ms
```

## Preserve the boundary

Most production failures happen at a boundary: one system assumes a shape, another system returns a different one. A good log records the boundary and the decision taken there.
